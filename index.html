The Vasuki engineering suite

Six tools.
Clear, distinct jobs.

Each application is designed around a specific project task. Together they reduce repetitive work while keeping inputs, exceptions, and engineering decisions visible.

01 / BIM
Model automation

Revit Plug-in

A Revit-native command suite for annotation, electrical raceway schedules, calculations, and controlled model production.

  • Template-based Smart Annotation across 57 host categories
  • Collision, crop, tag, and leader protection
  • Duct-bank annotation and professional tag arrangement
  • RCT schedules with PDF and Excel export
R2 · Autodesk Revit 2025
Product ownershipCV Engineering
02 / CAD
Drawing standardization

CAD Plug-in

Batch drawing preparation for consistent page setup, clean paper-space output, and controlled drafting standards.

  • Batch page setup across open DWGs and paper layouts
  • A0–A4, ARCH, landscape, centering, and plot control
  • Viewport expansion, locking, and non-plot borders
  • Grayscale cleanup while protecting external references
Full AutoCAD 2022–2027
Product ownershipSiddireddy Naga Srinivasu
03 / PDF
Engineering PDF workspace

PDF Engine

A desktop PDF workspace for clear viewing, fast search, precise engineering markup, and dependable annotation round trips.

  • High-fidelity rendering with searchable text
  • Text, callouts, stamps, snapshots, and editable markups
  • Save and Save As with annotation preservation
  • Bluebeam-aware comment interoperability
Standalone desktop engine
Product ownershipSiddireddy Naga Srinivasu
04 / QA
Cable termination QA / QC

Termination Checker

Cross-check cable lists against electrical schematics and keep every proposed termination tied to drawing-page evidence.

  • Excel, CSV, searchable PDF, and scanned-PDF OCR
  • EPLAN continuations and terminal-first extraction
  • Core lists, drawing references, and clickable PDF pages
  • PASS, REVIEW, NOT FOUND, and possible missing cables
Evidence-backed review workflow
Product ownershipSiddireddy Naga Srinivasu
05 / LUX
Offline photometric design

Lighting Calculator

A fully offline IES calculation workspace for rooms, irregular boundaries, CAD-referenced plans, and large industrial areas.

  • Multiple IES files with manufacturer and test data
  • DXF backgrounds, snapping, dimensions, and room tracing
  • Automatic layouts plus precise manual batch editing
  • Lux maps, isolux contours, BOQ, and PDF reports
Offline calculation workspace
Product ownershipSiddireddy Naga Srinivasu
06 / 3D
Electrical panel engineering

Vasuki Panel Studio

An offline-first workspace for turning EPLAN PDF information and manufacturer STEP models into checked 3D mounting layouts.

  • Multi-page PDF assisted enclosure-dimension analysis
  • STEP/STP geometry at millimetre scale
  • DIN rails, cable ducts, grid snap, and six mounting faces
  • Overlap checks, project JSON, and 3D layout PDF
Offline Windows application
Product ownershipSiddireddy Naga Srinivasu

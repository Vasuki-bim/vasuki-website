Core Platform

Electrical BIM workflows that feel engineered, not improvised.

Vasuki brings frequently used electrical calculations, Revit model automation, Excel comparison, and report preparation into one focused toolset for electrical designers, BIM modelers, and project engineers.

Electrical Calculators

Fast calculators for power, voltage, current, horsepower, lighting levels, cable sizing, conduit fill, and cable tray sizing.

  • Free tools for day-to-day engineering checks
  • Designed to reduce spreadsheet switching
  • Clear results for engineer review and record keeping

SNS Automation Commands

Licensed automation commands for high-impact Revit workflows where consistent model data and repeatable output matter.

  • Conduit and cable tray numbering
  • DWG number synchronization
  • Grounding identification text workflows

Cross Checking and QA

Comparison tools help detect revisions between old and new datasets before the change reaches drawings, schedules, or site documentation.

  • Excel-driven cable length validation
  • Added, modified, and removed item tracking
  • Summary output for quick revision review
